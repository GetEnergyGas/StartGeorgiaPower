  • Same-day option: Available in select areas with early morning requests
  • What you need: Address, move-in date, ID, phone number

How to Start Georgia Power Service: Step-by-Step Process

Follow these verified steps to activate Georgia Power electricity in your home:

Step 1: Verify Address Eligibility

Go to georgiapower.com and enter your full service address (street number, street name, apartment number, city, state, ZIP code).

Step 2: Select Your Start Date

Choose your move-in date. This is the date you want electricity activated. For same-day requests, submit early in the morning (before 10 AM EST). Georgia Power processes requests more quickly early in the business day.

Step 3: Complete Verification

Provide identity verification information. This typically includes your name, date of birth, SSN (last 4 digits), state ID number, or driver's license. Georgia Power uses this to prevent fraud.

Step 4: Submit Your Request

Submit your service start request online, by phone (1-856-900-7834), or in person at a Georgia Power office. You'll receive a confirmation number via email or text. Save this confirmation number.

Step 5: Confirm Activation on Move-In Day

On your start date, check your home's main meter and breaker box. Power should be active. If electricity is not on, call Georgia Power immediately with your confirmation number.

Pro Tip: Use the exact same address as shown on your lease, purchase agreement, or utility bill. Mismatched address information is the #1 cause of activation delays.

Can You Get Same-Day Georgia Power Activation?

Yes, but only in select areas and with timing. Same-day Georgia Power connections are possible if all of these conditions are met:

  • ✓ Your address is within a service area with available same-day capacity
  • ✓ Your request is submitted before 10 AM Eastern Standard Time
  • ✓ All verification information is accurate and verifiable
  • ✓ There is no active previous account at the address
  • ✓ No credit flags or security holds are present
  • ✓ You complete the process the morning of your start date

To request same-day activation: Call Georgia Power at 1-856-900-7834 early in the morning and explicitly request same-day service. Have your address, move-in date, and ID information ready.

⚠️ Same-day availability is not guaranteed. Capacity is limited and varies by location. Even if you request early, Georgia Power may not confirm same-day activation if demand is high.

Why Georgia Power Service May Not Start: Troubleshooting Common Problems

Issue: "Address not found" or "service not available"

Cause: Address formatting error, rural address outside service territory, or apartment unit number missing.
Solution: Confirm address with lease documents. Include apt/unit number. Try entering just the street address without unit. Call Georgia Power to manually verify service eligibility.

Issue: Verification failed or "identity cannot be verified"

Cause: Name, DOB, or SSN mismatch; credit report issues; or address mismatch with ID.
Solution: Use the exact name on your ID. Check spelling carefully. If credit issues block service, ask for prepaid meter option. Consider in-person verification at a Georgia Power office.

Issue: Previous account still shows active

Cause: Former occupant hasn't closed their account or requested a transfer.
Solution: Ask Georgia Power if a transfer is possible (faster). If not, have the previous occupant formally close their account. This typically takes 24-48 hours, then you can activate new service.

Issue: No power on move-in day (activation failed)

Cause: System delay, verification still pending, meter issue, or breaker not reset.
Solution: (1) Check your home's breaker box—a breaker may be off. (2) Verify the meter is connected. (3) Call Georgia Power immediately with your confirmation number.

Issue: High deposit or prepaid meter required

Cause: Credit check showed issues or new customer with no utility history.
Solution: Ask about the deposit amount and terms. Most deposits are refunded after 12 months of on-time payments. Prepaid meters allow service without a deposit but require upfront payment.

What You Need to Start Georgia Power Service

Required Information

  • Full service address: Street number, street name, apt/unit number, city, state, ZIP code
  • Move-in date: The date you want power activated (cannot be more than 30 days away)
  • Phone number: For confirmation and contact purposes
  • Email address: For delivery of confirmation and account details
  • Proof of address: Lease, purchase agreement, or utility bill showing the service address

Identity Verification (one of the following)

  • Driver's license or state ID (photo ID)
  • Last 4 digits of SSN + date of birth
  • Passport number
  • In-person verification with photo ID at a Georgia Power office

Optional but Helpful

  • Account or confirmation number from previous address (speeds verification)
  • Landlord contact info (if renting)
  • Previous occupant's account info (helps if prior account still open)

Frequently Asked Questions About Starting Georgia Power Service

Q: How do I check the status of my Georgia Power activation?

A: Log into your account at georgiapower.com using your confirmation number or email. Or call 1-856-900-7834 with your confirmation number. Georgia Power will provide real-time activation status.

Q: Can I change my start date after submitting my request?

A: Yes, call Georgia Power immediately with your confirmation number. Changes are usually free if requested before your original start date. You can move the date forward or backward up to 30 days.

Q: What if I need power activated urgently (emergency)?

A: Call Georgia Power at 1-888-500-4328 and ask for an emergency or expedited start. Explain your situation. They may be able to activate service within hours if capacity is available.

Q: Is there a fee to start Georgia Power service?

A: No startup fee, but Georgia Power may require a security deposit (typically $75–$300) depending on credit. Prepaid meter customers pay upfront for electricity used.

Q: Can I start service online, or do I have to call?

A: You can start online at georgiapower.com/start-stop-service. Phone requests (1-856-900-7834) and in-person applications are also available.

Q: What happens if I don't provide a move-in date for electricity?

A: Your request may be delayed or cancelled. Georgia Power requires a specific start date. Submit a date within the next 30 days. You can always adjust it later.
