Start Georgia Power Service – Setup, Activation & Same-Day Electricity

Georgia Power service can be started online or through a service request using your address and move-in date. Most activations take 1–3 business days, while same-day service may be available depending on request timing.

Georgia Power primarily provides electricity service across most regions of Georgia, including residential, commercial, and rental properties. However, service availability depends on your exact address and local distribution coverage.

In some areas, service may already be active under a previous occupant, or may require transfer instead of a new connection.

  • Coverage may vary in rural or newly developed locations
