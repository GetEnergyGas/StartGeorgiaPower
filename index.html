Quick answer: start Georgia Power service today

Request Georgia Power service online or by phone. Typical activation is 1–3 business days; same‑day connection may be possible in some areas if the request and verification are completed early. Provide your complete service address and move‑in date to avoid delays.

How to start Georgia Power service: step‑by‑step guide

  1. Confirm address eligibility — enter the full service address (street, unit, city, ZIP).
  2. Choose start date — pick your move‑in date; same‑day requests must be early.
  3. Provide verification — identity or account verification may be required.
  4. Submit the request — online at Georgia Power or by phone; save confirmation details.
  5. Verify activation — on move‑in day, check meters and contact support if power is not active.

Use exact address formatting to avoid verification delays. If a previous occupant still has service, request a transfer instead of a new connection.

Why your Georgia Power service may not start — common causes

  • Incorrect or incomplete address details
  • Verification not completed
  • Previous occupant still shows active service
  • High demand on move‑in days

If power is not active on move‑in day, confirm request status and contact support immediately. Keep confirmation numbers and timestamps for escalation.

Frequently asked questions — Georgia Power service start

How do I start Georgia Power service online or by phone?

Start service online at Georgia Power's official site or call the utility. Provide your full address and move‑in date and complete any required verification.

Can I get same‑day electricity from Georgia Power?

Same‑day connections may be available depending on location and timing. Submit requests early and complete verification to increase the chance of same‑day activation.

How long does it take to connect Georgia Power service?

Most activations complete within 1–3 business days. Verification issues or address problems can extend this time.
