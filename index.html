How to Start Georgia Power Service: Step-by-Step Process

Follow these verified steps to activate Georgia Power electricity in your home:

Step 1: Verify Address Eligibility

Go to georgiapower.com and enter your full service address (street number, street name, unit number if applicable, city, and ZIP code). Georgia Power will confirm if service is available at your location. Keep note of your service address for your records.

Step 2: Select Your Start Date

Choose your move-in date. This is the date you want electricity activated. For same-day requests, submit early in the morning (before 10 AM EST). Georgia Power processes requests in the order received during business hours.

Step 3: Complete Verification

Provide identity verification information. This typically includes your name, date of birth, SSN (last 4 digits), state ID number, or driver's license. Georgia Power uses this to prevent fraud and confirm you are authorized to open an account at this address.

Step 4: Submit Your Request

Submit your service start request online, by phone (1-888-500-4328), or in person at a Georgia Power office. You'll receive a confirmation number via email or text. Save this confirmation number—you'll need it to track your request status.

Step 5: Confirm Activation on Move-In Day

On your start date, check your home's main meter and breaker box. Power should be active. If electricity is not on, call Georgia Power immediately with your confirmation number. Request an urgent status check or same-day technician visit if promised.

Pro Tip: Use the exact same address as shown on your lease, purchase agreement, or utility bill. Mismatched address information is the #1 cause of activation delays.

Can You Get Same-Day Georgia Power Activation?

Yes, but only in select areas and with timing. Same-day Georgia Power connections are possible if all of these conditions are met:

  • ✓ Your address is within a service area with available same-day capacity
  • ✓ Your request is submitted before 10 AM Eastern Standard Time
  • ✓ All verification information is accurate and verifiable
  • ✓ There is no active previous account at the address
  • ✓ No credit flags or security holds are present
  • ✓ You complete the process the morning of your start date

To request same-day activation: Call Georgia Power at 1-888-500-4328 early in the morning and explicitly request same-day service. Have your address, move-in date, and ID ready. Ask if same-day is available in your area before providing other information.

⚠️ Same-day availability is not guaranteed. Capacity is limited and varies by location. Even if you request early, Georgia Power may not confirm same-day activation until after verification is complete. Never assume same-day service without confirmation from the utility.

Why Georgia Power Service May Not Start: Troubleshooting Common Problems

Issue: "Address not found" or "service not available"

Cause: Address formatting error, rural address outside service territory, or apartment unit number missing.
Solution: Confirm address with lease documents. Include apt/unit number. Try entering just the street address without unit. Call Georgia Power to manually verify service availability.

Issue: Verification failed or "identity cannot be verified"

Cause: Name, DOB, or SSN mismatch; credit report issues; or address mismatch with ID.
Solution: Use the exact name on your ID. Check spelling carefully. If credit issues block service, ask for prepaid meter option. Consider in-person verification at a Georgia Power office with photo ID and address proof.

Issue: Previous account still shows active

Cause: Former occupant hasn't closed their account or requested a transfer.
Solution: Ask Georgia Power if a transfer is possible (faster). If not, have the previous occupant formally close their account. This typically takes 24-48 hours, then you can request a new account.

Issue: No power on move-in day (activation failed)

Cause: System delay, verification still pending, meter issue, or breaker not reset.
Solution: (1) Check your home's breaker box—a breaker may be off. (2) Verify the meter is connected. (3) Call Georgia Power immediately with your confirmation number. (4) Request an urgent technician visit. Document the time you called and the representative's name. (5) Contact the Georgia Public Service Commission if not resolved same day.

Issue: High deposit or prepaid meter required

Cause: Credit check showed issues or new customer with no utility history.
Solution: Ask about the deposit amount and terms. Most deposits are refunded after 12 months of on-time payments. Prepaid meters allow service without a deposit but require an upfront payment for usage. Compare options with Georgia Power.

What You Need to Start Georgia Power Service

Required Information

  • Full service address: Street number, street name, apt/unit number, city, state, ZIP code
  • Move-in date: The date you want power activated (cannot be more than 30 days away)
  • Phone number: For confirmation and contact purposes
  • Email address: For delivery of confirmation and account details
  • Proof of address: Lease, purchase agreement, or utility bill showing the service address

Identity Verification (one of the following)

  • Driver's license or state ID (photo ID)
  • Last 4 digits of SSN + date of birth
  • Passport number
  • In-person verification with photo ID at a Georgia Power office

Optional but Helpful

  • Account or confirmation number from previous address (speeds verification)
  • Landlord contact info (if renting)
  • Previous occupant's account info (helps if prior account still open)

Frequently Asked Questions About Starting Georgia Power Service

Q: How do I check the status of my Georgia Power activation?

A: Log into your account at georgiapower.com using your confirmation number or email. Or call 1-888-500-4328 with your confirmation number. Georgia Power will provide your activation status and expected activation date.

Q: Can I change my start date after submitting my request?

A: Yes, call Georgia Power immediately with your confirmation number. Changes are usually free if requested before your original start date. You can move the date forward or backward (within 30 days of the original request).

Q: What if I need power activated urgently (emergency)?

A: Call Georgia Power at 1-888-500-4328 and ask for an emergency or expedited start. Explain your situation. They may be able to activate service within hours if capacity and verification allow. Same-day service is more likely with emergency requests if submitted early.

Q: Is there a fee to start Georgia Power service?

A: No startup fee, but Georgia Power may require a security deposit (typically $75–$300) depending on credit. Prepaid meter customers pay upfront for electricity usage. Ask about deposit terms and payment options when requesting service.

Q: Can I start service online, or do I have to call?

A: You can start online at georgiapower.com/start-stop-service.html. Online requests are processed the same as phone requests. Some prefer calling to confirm same-day availability first.

Q: What happens if the previous occupant's account is still open?

A: Georgia Power cannot activate your account until the previous account closes. Ask Georgia Power if they can request a transfer (faster, usually 1 day). Or contact the previous occupant directly to close their account. Once closed, your activation can proceed normally.

Q: Do I need to visit a Georgia Power office in person to start service?

A: No, most customers complete the process online or by phone. In-person visits are only needed if verification fails online or for special circumstances (prepaid meter enrollment, deposit payment, etc.).

Q: How do I know my power is actually on when I move in?

A: (1) Check that your main breaker is in the ON position. (2) Turn on a light or appliance to confirm power. (3) Look at your meter to confirm it's spinning/counting usage. If nothing works, call Georgia Power immediately from a neighbor's phone or mobile.

Q: What if I'm renting? Does my landlord need to open the account?

A: No, you can open an account in your name as the occupant/renter. If the landlord pays for utilities, they open the account instead. Check your lease to see who is responsible for opening and paying Georgia Power. Confirm with your landlord before submitting your request.

Q: Is Georgia Power the only electricity provider in Georgia?

A: Georgia Power is the largest utility in Georgia, but it doesn't serve all areas. Some areas have alternative providers (municipal utilities, cooperatives, or deregulated providers in parts of the state). Check service availability at your address using Georgia Power's lookup tool to confirm service area.
